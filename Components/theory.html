Our Theory and Theorist

Two-Factor Theory

Herzberg proposed that job satisfaction and dissatisfaction are caused by different things. Motivators such as achievement, recognition and growth make people satisfied. Hygiene factors such as pay, policies and working conditions only stop people from being unhappy.
