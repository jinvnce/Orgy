Organizational Commitment

At COOL IT, our dedication to product innovation and employee fulfillment drives every FLUVB mini fan we create.

We believe a good product starts with a good team. FLUVB mini fans are built to give people effortless, everyday comfort, especially in a tropical climate where staying cool matters.

That same care applies to the people behind the product. We aim to run a workplace where members are treated fairly, recognized for their work, and given room to grow.

Below is where we want to go (our vision), how we get there (our mission), and how we look after our team.

Vision

To become the most trusted and innovative portable cooling brand in the Philippines and beyond.

We want to redefine everyday personal comfort through smart, sustainable, and stylish technology built for tropical living.

Mission

Under the COOL IT organization, FLUVB is dedicated to engineering high-velocity, long-lasting mini fans that deliver effortless relief anytime, anywhere.

Our People

Just as we prioritize comfort for our customers, we champion fulfillment for our team by applying Frederick Herzberg’s Two-Factor Theory.

We eliminate workplace dissatisfaction through fair, supportive standards, and we fuel excellence through recognition, autonomy, and meaningful growth.
