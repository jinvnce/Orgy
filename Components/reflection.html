Why this subject matters

Studying Organizational Communication is essential because it reveals how people actually coordinate work, navigate power, and make decisions inside a structured group. Rather than treating communication as simply transmitting information from sender to receiver, the subject examines how formal hierarchies, informal networks, and interpersonal trust determine whether teams align or stall. Ultimately, an organization is built and sustained through the everyday conversations and habits of its members. Understanding these dynamics equips us to diagnose bottlenecks, foster psychological safety, and intentionally shape a healthier, more effective workplace culture.
