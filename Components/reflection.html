Organizations run on everyday conversations, not just org charts.

Organizational Communication shows how people coordinate work, handle power, and make decisions in a group. It treats communication as more than sending a message from sender to receiver.

Understanding these dynamics helps us find bottlenecks, build psychological safety, and shape a healthier workplace culture on purpose.

Formal hierarchies

Clear roles and chains of command decide who coordinates with whom.

Informal networks

Everyday talk and shared habits decide whether a team stays aligned or stalls.

Interpersonal trust

Trust and safety let members speak up, share ideas, and fix problems early.
